# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

Welcome to the OctoAcme project management docs. This is your central hub for understanding how we run projects, manage teams, and deliver results.

## Quick Overview

OctoAcme follows a structured, customer-first approach to project management centered around five core lifecycle phases:

1. **Initiation** — Validate business need, align stakeholders, define success metrics
2. **Planning** — Break work into shippable increments, identify dependencies, align timelines
3. **Execution** — Build, test, review, and iterate with clear quality gates
4. **Release** — Deploy with confidence, verify outcomes, and communicate progress
5. **Close & Retrospective** — Capture learnings and drive continuous improvement

## Core Principles

- **Customer-first**: Prioritize customer value and usability
- **Iterative delivery**: Deliver small, testable increments
- **Clear ownership**: Each project has named PM and Product Lead roles
- **Data-informed**: Measure impact and iterate based on evidence
- **Psychological safety**: Encourage feedback and learning

## OctoAcme Project Management Approach

OctoAcme's project management practices emphasize structured phases, clear role separation, and integrated communication. The organization operates with explicit decision gates at each lifecycle phase to ensure business need, stakeholder alignment, and measurable success metrics are defined before work begins. A dual PM/PdM model—where Project Managers coordinate delivery and manage risks while Product Managers drive outcomes and prioritization—prevents bottlenecks and clarifies decision ownership.

Communication and risk management are woven throughout execution via a regular cadence of daily standups (15 min), weekly delivery syncs, and monthly stakeholder updates. A formal escalation path (Team → PM → Product Lead → Sponsor) ensures risks and dependencies are triaged at the right level. Quality is enforced through both process and automation: unit and integration tests are required for new logic, pull requests are kept to ≤400 lines and require at least one approval, security scanning runs in CI, and pre-release checklists ensure smoke tests and rollback plans are documented before deployment. This combination of lightweight phases, clear roles, proactive communication, and automated quality gates enables OctoAcme to deliver customer value safely and iteratively.
